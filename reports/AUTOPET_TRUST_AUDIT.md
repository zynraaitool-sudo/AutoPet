# AutoPet Trust Audit

## Existing protections

- Product pages explicitly state that seller listings have not been independently tested.
- Affiliate and direct links receive appropriate link attributes and disclosures.
- Review schema is not fabricated.
- Article schema uses editorial Article markup without invented ratings or offers.

## Improvements

- Product pages now separate listed features from practical verification needs.
- Buying sections avoid claims about testing, certifications, or safety guarantees.
- Review pages include clear links to product listings and category resources.

## Remaining trust work

- Verify exact manufacturer specifications where they are presented by the seller.
- Confirm author identity and editorial-review process only with project-supported evidence.
- Keep prices and availability as variable listing details, not as guarantees.
