Window mesh can be part of a broader home-safety setup, but an unsecured screen can create a false sense of security. The AliExpress listing describes protective mesh; AutoPet has not tested tear resistance or load capacity.

## Measure the opening and review the package

Measure the full opening, including overlap needed at the frame. Check the roll's width and length, mesh material, edge treatment, and all included fasteners. Ask the seller how it is intended to attach to your particular frame. Some window styles may need a rigid frame or a professionally installed barrier rather than a loose net.

## Inspect the installation regularly

Make sure all edges are attached without gaps and that the frame cannot be pushed or pulled out. Test the window's normal operation without a cat present, and keep the opening closed until the barrier is complete. Recheck fasteners and mesh for fraying, stretching, or damage. Never rely on the mesh alone at an unsecured open window.

## Bottom line

Treat mesh as one component, not a safety certification. If the installation method is uncertain, consult a qualified installer and keep the window closed.
