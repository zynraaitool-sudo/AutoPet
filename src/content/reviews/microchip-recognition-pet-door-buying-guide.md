## Check compatibility before cutting

Obtain the exact chip frequencies or standards from the door documentation and compare them with the pet’s implant record. Confirm whether the reader is built into the door, whether a separate tag is required, and how many chips can be registered. The source gives no supported chip types, registration capacity, or reader range.

Measure the pet at shoulder height and determine the correct opening width and height. The source does not provide flap dimensions or recommended pet sizes. Also verify the door thickness and wall or panel type at the installation location; cutting should wait until an exact template and all mounting parts are confirmed.

## Understand the lock and power needs

Confirm the four-way lock states, flap operation, batteries or power supply, and behavior if power or batteries fail. Ask whether entry and exit can be set independently and whether a manual lock is available. Make sure every household pet can be identified if the same setting is intended for multiple animals.

Before permanent installation, perform a dry fitting or temporary setup if the seller’s instructions allow it. Test recognition while the pet approaches normally, and watch for hesitation, repeated flap contact, or unintended entry and exit.

## Common mistakes

Do not cut the opening before chip and size compatibility are confirmed. Do not assume every microchip or tag works. Do not rely on the lock without checking what happens during low battery or loss of power.
