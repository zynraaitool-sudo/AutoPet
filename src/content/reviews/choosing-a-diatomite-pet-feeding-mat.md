The AliExpress listing presents a pet feeding-area mat and mentions diatomite and a non-slip design. AutoPet has not inspected the product or verified its composition. The material name alone does not establish how it absorbs water, how it should be cleaned, or whether it is suitable for a particular floor.

## Verify what the product is made of

Check the complete description, dimensions, underside material, edge finish, and whether the item is rigid or flexible. Ask the seller for clarification if the listing uses conflicting material or water-resistance descriptions. Compare the product option with the photos and specifications before checkout.

## Check the surface and care requirements

Look for clear instructions on cleaning, drying, and storage. Do not use detergents or soaking methods unless the seller says they are appropriate. Inspect the mat when it arrives for chips, cracks, rough edges, or loose pieces.

## Place it safely

Test the underside on the intended floor and check that the mat stays in place when a bowl is nudged. Keep it where your pet cannot chew it. Remove it if it breaks or if material begins to come loose.

Before ordering, compare the actual size with the feeding area and check the package contents, shipping, and return policy. Treat “non-slip” and material claims as seller descriptions until independently verified.
