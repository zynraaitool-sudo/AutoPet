An elevated window seat may offer a cat a place to rest, but a suction-mounted accessory needs careful assessment. The AliExpress listing describes a window hammock; AutoPet has not tested its cups, frame, or load capacity.

## Check the window and package

Read the installation steps and identify the required surface: smooth glass, frame, or another support. Confirm the selected variant includes every bracket, cord, or cushion shown. Check measurements against the usable window area and the cat's size. Do not infer a maximum safe weight from product imagery or generic marketplace wording.

## Install and monitor conservatively

Clean and dry the surface as directed, then install the perch without stretching or modifying parts. Keep the area below clear and avoid windows where a fall could reach stairs, sharp furniture, or an exterior opening. Inspect every attachment before use and at regular intervals, especially after temperature changes or cleaning. Remove the perch if a cup loosens or a support becomes damaged.

## Bottom line

The safest choice is not just the hammock that fits the window; it is a setup with sound supports, regular checks, and a low-risk location. Never leave a cat near an unsecured open window.
