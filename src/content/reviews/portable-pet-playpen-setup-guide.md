A portable playpen may create a temporary boundary for a pet, but its materials and stability vary. The AliExpress listing describes a foldable mesh enclosure; AutoPet has not tested whether it resists climbing, chewing, or tipping.

## Check fit and contents

Compare the assembled dimensions with the pet's size and movement needs. Check if a floor panel, cover, carry bag, and stakes are included in the selected option. Inspect zippers, seams, support rods, and corners on arrival. Ensure the pen is large enough for the animal to change position comfortably and that ventilation remains unobstructed.

## Set up for supervised use

Assemble on a flat, clear surface and gently check the frame for movement. Keep the enclosure away from stairs, heaters, sharp edges, and other hazards. Stay nearby and remove the pet if it chews the mesh, climbs, or appears distressed. Discontinue use if a seam tears or a support bends.

## Bottom line

Treat a playpen as a supervised short-term aid, not secure unattended housing. Confirm the full package and inspect it before every use.
