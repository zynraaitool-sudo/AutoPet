## Check the contents

Request a complete itemized list and compare it with the delivered package. Check packaging integrity, storage conditions, and expiry dates for any consumable items. Avoid assuming that a product labeled "first aid" contains appropriate or sterile supplies. Ask your veterinarian what should be kept at home or in a travel bag for your pet.

## Plan for urgent care

Store emergency contact details, your veterinarian's number, and the nearest emergency clinic information with the kit. Learn basic handling only from qualified veterinary sources. Do not administer medicines or attempt a procedure based solely on a kit insert or online listing. For severe bleeding, breathing trouble, poisoning concerns, or collapse, contact emergency veterinary care immediately.

## Bottom line

Buy only after verifying contents and consult a veterinarian about its use. A kit is an organizational aid, not a substitute for professional care.
