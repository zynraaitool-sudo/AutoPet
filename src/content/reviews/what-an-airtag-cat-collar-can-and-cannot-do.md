This AliExpress listing describes a reflective cat collar with a holder for an Apple AirTag, and mentions a bell. AutoPet has not tested the collar. Check whether the tracker itself is included, which collar size is selected, and whether the holder fits the current AirTag model.

## Know the technology's limits

An AirTag is not a standalone GPS device and does not guarantee real-time location updates. Its location reporting depends on Apple's Find My network and nearby compatible devices. Do not rely on it as the only way to identify or locate a cat.

## Fit and release matter

Confirm the collar circumference range, buckle design, and whether it has a suitable quick-release or breakaway feature. Make sure the tracker holder does not make the collar heavy or stiff for your cat. Check that the bell and holder are secure and do not create snag hazards.

## Use it as one part of a plan

Keep identification details current and follow local guidance for pet identification. Inspect the collar regularly for wear, fit, and irritation. Remove it if it becomes damaged or catches on objects.

Before purchase, review the package contents, size chart, tracker compatibility, return terms, and current listing. Reflective trim may improve visibility in some lighting, but it does not replace supervision.
