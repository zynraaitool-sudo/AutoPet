## Details to confirm

Before ordering, check the exact model and option, reservoir capacity, pump and power requirements, filter type, replacement-filter availability, and cleaning instructions. Confirm whether the bowl and pump can be disassembled as directed and which parts must stay dry. Product images may show accessories that are not included in every option.

Keep a separate clean water source available while introducing a fountain. Follow the manufacturer's cleaning schedule, and check water level and operation regularly.

## Bottom line

The listing provides a starting point for comparing material and intended use, but not enough information to determine whether the fountain fits your household. Verify the exact parts, capacity, maintenance needs, and seller terms on the current page. This is not a hands-on review.
