A clicker can provide a distinct marker sound during reward-based training. The AliExpress listing describes a combined clicker and whistle, but AutoPet has not tested its sound level or reliability.

## Check the device

Confirm whether the product has separate controls for the click and whistle, what attachments are included, and whether the sound can be used comfortably indoors. Choose a volume that does not frighten the dog. Inspect the housing for sharp edges or loose parts and store the tool out of reach when not in use.

## Use a consistent marker

In a quiet setting, make the sound and immediately offer a small reward. Repeat a few times before using it to mark a simple behavior the dog already performs. Mark once at the moment of the behavior, then reward; timing and consistency are more useful than repeated clicks. Stop if the dog seems worried by the sound.

## Bottom line

The tool is optional. A verbal marker works too, and training should remain calm, brief, and rewarding.
