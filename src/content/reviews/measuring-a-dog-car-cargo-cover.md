The AliExpress listing gives dimensions of 137 × 147 cm and describes the cover as waterproof. AutoPet has not tested it in a vehicle. Before ordering, check whether those dimensions refer to the full cover and whether the selected option includes all shown straps.

## Measure the real coverage area

Measure the width and depth of the seat or cargo floor where the cover will rest. Include the shape of the side walls, seat backs, headrests, and any gap between surfaces. Check whether the cover is intended for a rear seat, a trunk, or both; a broad product title may group several options together.

## Check attachment and access

Find out how the cover attaches and whether its straps fit your vehicle's headrests. Make sure seat-belt buckles remain accessible if a pet restraint uses them. Loose fabric should not interfere with the driver, pedals, doors, or the dog's footing.

## Do not confuse protection with restraint

A cover may help protect upholstery from dirt, but it does not secure a dog during sudden braking. Use a separate, appropriate restraint according to its instructions and your vehicle's guidance. Do not attach a restraint to a cover unless the restraint maker explicitly says that is an approved anchor.

## Confirm care and current terms

Check cleaning instructions, seams, dimensions, the exact variant, shipping, and returns. Test the fit while parked before taking a trip, and adjust or remove the cover if it slides or blocks access to safety equipment.
