The ROJECO stainless steel fountain is a budget-friendly option sold through AliExpress. This is a buyer-based review: we have not lab-tested it, so everything below comes from the listing and customer reviews. Treat product claims as the seller’s claims until you confirm them.

### What the listing says

The listing describes a 3.2L (108 oz) capacity, a pump described as ultra-quiet, and a body made of SUS304 stainless steel and ABS plastic. It is sold as a fountain for cats and dogs and comes in several bundles, including a basic set and sets with extra filters. The seller is ROJECO Choice Store, and the listing shows free shipping, delivery within roughly a week to ten days in most cases, and a free-returns window.

One caution: the summary shown on the page is AI-generated and the page itself says it does not represent the merchant’s opinion. Details like the noise level are worth confirming with real buyer feedback.

### What buyers say

At the time of writing the product had a 4.8 rating from 53 ratings, all marked as verified purchases.

**Positive feedback**

* Buyers describe the fountain as quiet and easy to use, and several mention fast delivery.
* One buyer liked that stainless steel does not need polishing products.
* Another said the sloped central design avoids the metallic dripping sound some fountains make.
* A buyer reported that their cat took to it quickly and described the build as sturdy.

**Complaints to know about**

* **Shipping damage:**

   one buyer received it damaged, another said the box arrived dented although the product was fine, and a third said the base was slightly damaged in transit. Check the package when it arrives and photograph any damage right away.
* **No wall plug:**

   one buyer says it comes with a USB cable but no adapter for a wall socket, so you may need to buy one separately. Check the listing for your region before ordering.
* **No water-level window:**

   a buyer noted you cannot see the water level, which makes it harder to know when to refill.
* **Noise:**

   one buyer found it a bit louder than their previous fountain, so “quiet” is relative.
* One reviewer recommended the version without the motion sensor, because they feel constant recirculation keeps the water fresher.

* Cleaning and filters
* Stainless steel is easy to wipe down, and the listing includes filter sets. Replace filters on the schedule the seller recommends, and wash all parts regularly, since no fountain stays clean without upkeep.
* Who it suits
* It fits owners who want a simple, app-free fountain at a modest price and are comfortable with AliExpress shipping. If you want hydration tracking, an app, or a local warranty, a branded model from a regular pet retailer is a better fit.

### Pros and cons

**Pros**

* Stainless steel body
* Mostly positive verified reviews
* Filter bundles available
* No app or subscription

**Cons**

* Several reports of shipping damage
* May need a separate USB wall adapter
* No visible water-level window
* Quietness is claimed, not independently measured

### Our take

For a simple, plug-in stainless steel fountain, the ROJECO looks like a reasonable low-cost pick, as long as you accept AliExpress shipping and inspect the parcel on arrival. Prices and bundles change, so check the current listing before buying.
