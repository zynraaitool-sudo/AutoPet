This AliExpress listing is presented as a fully automatic, app-controlled litter box with a large-capacity design. AutoPet has not tested the product, and “large” is not a substitute for measurements or a maintenance plan.

## Ask for measurements, not adjectives

Find the complete footprint, the usable area inside the unit, entry height, and waste-bin volume. Compare these with the room and your cat's size and mobility. Check whether the opening or rotating mechanism changes the usable space. If the listing shows multiple versions, make sure the product details correspond to the option you intend to buy.

## Clarify what automation includes

The title does not establish how the cleaning cycle starts, what sensors stop it, or how the unit handles interrupted power. Look for a product manual that explains safety features, cycle timing, reset behavior, and troubleshooting. Confirm which litter types are compatible and whether bags, liners, or other consumables are proprietary.

## Understand the app and routine

Ask which app is used, what phone or network requirements apply, and whether local controls remain available offline. Notifications can be useful, but they do not prove the device has completed a cycle or that the waste drawer is ready to use.

## Introduce the box carefully

Keep the familiar litter tray available during transition and let the cat explore the new unit voluntarily. Do not start a cleaning cycle while the cat is investigating it. Continue inspecting and cleaning the device according to its instructions even if an app reports normal operation.

Before buying, check the selected variant, seller's return terms, replacement-part availability, and current delivery details. Treat the listing as a starting point for verification, not as evidence of hands-on performance.

## Common mistake to avoid

A common mistake is choosing by exterior dimensions rather than usable interior space. Separate stated seller details from independently tested results, and confirm unresolved points in the live listing or manufacturer's documentation.
