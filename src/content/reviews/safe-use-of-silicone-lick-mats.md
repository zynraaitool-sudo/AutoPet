## Choose an appropriate portion

Use only a food that is suitable for your pet. Count it as part of the pet's normal daily food rather than adding an unmeasured extra. If your pet has a prescribed diet or food sensitivities, follow that plan instead of using a generic product suggestion.

## Check the suction and surface

Test the cups on a clean, smooth surface that the seller says is compatible. Suction performance can vary with texture and moisture. Place the mat where it cannot slide into a walkway or be pulled down from a height.

## Supervise and clean thoroughly

Stay nearby while the pet uses the mat. Remove it if the animal chews it, tears off pieces, or becomes frustrated. Clean grooves and suction cups according to the seller's instructions, then dry the mat fully before storing it. Confirm dishwasher safety rather than assuming it from the word “silicone.”

Check whether the listing's package includes one mat or multiple accessories, and compare the product dimensions with the feeding area before ordering.
