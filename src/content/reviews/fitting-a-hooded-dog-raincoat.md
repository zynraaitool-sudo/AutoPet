The AliExpress listing describes an outdoor raincoat with a detachable hood and makes waterproof and wind-resistance claims. AutoPet has not tested it in rain. Confirm the fabric details, size chart, and closure design for the exact option.

## Measure instead of guessing by breed

Follow the seller's measurement guide for body length, chest, and neck. Compare your dog's measurements with the garment chart and allow enough room for normal movement. Do not choose a size based only on a breed label or estimated weight.

## Check the hood and harness access

The hood must not cover the dog's eyes, interfere with hearing, or restrict breathing. Check whether the coat has an opening for a collar or harness and whether straps or closures rub under the legs. A detached hood is useful only if the garment remains secure without it.

## Introduce it before a walk

Let your dog inspect the coat and try it briefly indoors. Watch for rubbing, restricted stride, or signs of stress. Remove it if the dog cannot move comfortably. Use a leash and harness as intended; do not attach walking gear to a coat unless the manufacturer explicitly says that is safe.

Before buying, confirm the variant, care instructions, current shipping, and returns. The listing's weather claims do not establish warmth or suitability for every climate.
