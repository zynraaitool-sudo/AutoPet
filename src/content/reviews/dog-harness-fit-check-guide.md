This guide summarizes a seller-listed dog walking harness. AutoPet has not fitted or tested the product, and the general size description in the title is not a substitute for the seller's measurements.

## Measure and compare

Use the seller's current size chart and measure the dog as instructed. Check the adjustment range at every listed measurement; do not select a size based only on breed or weight. If your dog falls between sizes, ask the seller which fit is appropriate and confirm the return policy.

Look at how the harness closes, where the leash attaches, and whether the strap layout can be adjusted without rubbing or restricting movement. Confirm the listed material and care instructions, and inspect stitching and hardware when the product arrives.

## Keep the intended use clear

The AliExpress listing describes a harness for walking. It does not establish crash testing or vehicle-restraint safety. Do not use a walking harness in a car as a safety restraint unless the manufacturer explicitly documents that exact use.

Introduce the harness gradually and check your dog's comfort during a short, supervised walk. Stop using it if it slips, causes rubbing, or shows damage.

## Bottom line

Fit and intended use matter more than a generic "medium and large" label. Verify measurements, adjustment points, construction, return terms, and the exact option before ordering.
