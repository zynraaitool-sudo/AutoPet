Combining a brush and rinse may simplify bath setup, but connector compatibility is product-specific. The AliExpress listing mentions a brush and hose; AutoPet has not tested its connection, pressure, or bristle softness.

## Check the connection and control

Find the connector type and diameter, and confirm whether adapters are included in the selected variant. Check if the tool has a shutoff or flow-control switch. Ask the seller how the brush disassembles for cleaning and drying. Avoid assuming that a garden-hose connector will fit a household shower.

## Make bath time gradual

Test the device away from the dog for leaks and water pressure. Use warm—not hot—water and begin with a gentle flow on an area the dog tolerates. Keep water away from eyes and ears, and stop if the dog becomes distressed. Rinse thoroughly and dry the brush after use.

## Bottom line

Buy only after checking the connector and care instructions. A grooming accessory should support calm handling, not force a bath.
