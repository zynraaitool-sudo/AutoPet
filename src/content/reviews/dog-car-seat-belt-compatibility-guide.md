## Verify the attachment

Identify whether the tether plugs into a seat-belt receptacle, loops around a headrest, or uses another anchor. Compare that connector with your vehicle and check if it can interfere with airbags, seat movement, or normal seat-belt use. Confirm the length and adjustability, and inspect stitching, clips, and release mechanisms. If the seller cannot clearly explain compatibility, do not guess.

## Use a harness, not a collar

A restraint tether should be attached only to equipment intended for that use and fitted according to its maker's instructions. Never attach it to a collar or use it to lift the dog. Check local requirements and look for independently verified crash-test information if crash protection is a priority.

## Bottom line

A tether can limit movement but cannot be assumed to protect occupants or an animal in a collision. Secure the dog using a compatible, properly fitted system and never allow the dog to ride loose.
