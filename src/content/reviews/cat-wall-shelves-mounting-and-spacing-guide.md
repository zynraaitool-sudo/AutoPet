Wall-mounted shelves can create vertical routes for indoor cats, but installation quality matters more than a catalog photo. The AliExpress listing is not an engineering specification; AutoPet has not tested its hardware or load capacity.

## Plan before drilling

Inventory the selected option and measure each platform, bracket, and climbing surface. Identify the wall construction and locate studs or suitable masonry anchors. Do not mount heavy or elevated pieces with fasteners that are incompatible with the wall. Plan a sequence of reachable steps, with a landing spot and no sharp furniture or glass below.

## Check after installation

Follow the assembly directions, then apply gentle pressure to every platform before allowing access. Recheck screws and brackets periodically and after the cat jumps onto the route. Stop use if any part shifts, cracks, or loosens. Provide a way down that does not force the cat to jump from a height.

## Bottom line

Confirm the kit contents and mounting requirements before purchase. If the wall or anchoring method is uncertain, get help from a qualified installer rather than improvising.
