A pet stroller can provide a way to travel when walking is limited, but its size and mechanics should fit both the pet and the route. AutoPet has not tested the AliExpress stroller or verified its listed load limit.

## Compare measurements and features

Measure the pet in a comfortable resting position and compare those dimensions with the interior cabin. Check folded dimensions against your storage space and vehicle. Confirm whether the option includes a removable carrier, rain cover, basket, or internal tether; images may show accessories that are sold separately. Ask for the wheel and brake details if the listing is unclear.

## Inspect before use

Assemble the stroller and test its brakes, locks, zippers, and wheel movement before putting a pet inside. Check ventilation and make sure the pet cannot slip through an opening. Start with a short, quiet indoor session, then use a level route and keep the stroller supervised.

## Bottom line

Choose by measured cabin fit, safe closures, and route suitability. Stop using it if the frame, brake, or wheel feels unstable.
