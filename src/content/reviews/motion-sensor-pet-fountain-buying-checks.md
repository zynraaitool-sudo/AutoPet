## Understand what “motion activated” means

Ask how close a pet must be to trigger the pump, how long the water flows, and whether the fountain has a continuous-flow setting. Check whether the sensor can be adjusted or turned off. Placement matters: a sensor facing a busy walkway may activate often, while an obstructed sensor may miss an approaching pet.

## Confirm the full power setup

USB power does not identify the required voltage, adapter, cable length, or whether the adapter is included. Confirm the manual's requirements and keep electrical connections away from splashes. Do not assume the fountain continues operating during a power failure.

## Check filters, capacity, and cleaning

Find the reservoir volume, filter model, replacement interval, and availability of replacement filters. Look for instructions on removing the pump and cleaning parts that contact water. A filter does not eliminate the need to wash the reservoir and refresh the water.

## Make the transition observable

Keep a separate clean water bowl available when introducing a new fountain. Watch whether your pet drinks from it and check that the pump runs, the water remains clean, and the sensor responds as expected. Do not rely on the listing's description as proof of performance.

Before buying, verify the selected option, seller instructions, current price, shipping, and return policy. The product title alone does not establish filter effectiveness or quiet operation.
