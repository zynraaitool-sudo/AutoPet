An exercise wheel can offer an optional indoor activity for a cat, but it is a large piece of equipment that needs room and stable assembly. The AliExpress listing describes the wheel as quiet; AutoPet has not tested its noise or balance.

## Measure the equipment and room

Check the wheel diameter, running-track width, base footprint, and total height. Confirm the delivered parts, assembly tools, and how the wheel is secured against tipping or excessive movement. Place it on a level surface with clear space around it and no nearby furniture the cat could strike.

## Let the cat decide

Allow the cat to explore the stationary wheel and reward calm interest. Do not put the cat inside or spin it to encourage use. Keep initial sessions short and supervise. Stop if the wheel shifts, squeaks unexpectedly, or the cat looks frightened. Inspect joints and track surface regularly.

## Bottom line

A wheel is optional enrichment, not a required exercise device. Buy only if you have room for a stable setup and the cat is comfortable exploring it.
