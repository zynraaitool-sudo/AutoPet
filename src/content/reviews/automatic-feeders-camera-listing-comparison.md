## What is stated in the listings

The first listing describes a 4L automatic feeder and mentions a camera, app control, and timed dry-food portions. The second title states a 7L capacity and two bowls, and mentions a camera, Tuya app control, remote monitoring, two-way audio, and timed feeding.

These are seller claims. A title does not confirm that every feature is included in every selectable option. It also does not tell us how accurately portions are dispensed, how the camera performs in low light, or how the app behaves during a network outage.

## How to compare the exact models

Before choosing either listing, check:

- The exact model and variant selected, including whether the camera and bowls are included.
- The usable food capacity and the food types or kibble sizes supported in the manual.
- How many meals and portion settings are available, and how portions are measured.
- Whether app access requires a particular Wi-Fi band, region, account, or subscription.
- How the two bowls are supplied: one shared portion, split output, or separately controlled servings.
- Power needs, any backup option, cleaning instructions, included accessories, shipping, and returns.

Do not rely on a feeder for a prescribed diet until its documented controls are confirmed with your veterinarian. Test the setup at home before depending on it for a scheduled meal.

## Bottom line

The listing titles make the advertised capacities and camera-related features easy to compare, but they do not prove accuracy, reliability, or suitability for a particular household.
