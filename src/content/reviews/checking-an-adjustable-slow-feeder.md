The AliExpress title for this set mentions two bowls, five height settings, and a slow-feeding layout. AutoPet has not tested the bowls. Those descriptions should be checked against measurements and the exact option selected.

## Measure the feeding position

Ask for the height of each setting and the inner bowl diameter and depth. Compare these with your pet's size and normal feeding posture. “Adjustable” does not tell you which heights are available or whether the stand locks securely.

## Check cleaning and materials

Confirm whether both bowls lift out, whether the base can be washed, and what the food-contact surfaces are made from. Look for cleaning instructions and any parts that must stay dry. Check whether the stand has non-slip feet and remains steady when a pet eats.

## Understand the slow-feeder claim

The listing does not say how much the pattern changes eating speed. Watch your pet during meals and remove the bowl if it causes frustration, chewing, or difficulty reaching food. A slow-feeder pattern should not replace measured portions or a feeding plan.

If a veterinarian has recommended a particular diet, texture, or pace, ask before changing bowls or feeding routines. Verify the selected configuration, package contents, seller terms, and current price before ordering.
