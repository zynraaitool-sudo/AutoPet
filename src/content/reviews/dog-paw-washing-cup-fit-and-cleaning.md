A paw-washing cup can help contain rinse water after a walk, but fit and cleanability vary by design. The AliExpress listing describes a manual cup with a silicone insert; AutoPet has not tested its cleaning performance or durability.

## Measure and inspect

Compare the cup's inner diameter and depth with the dog's paw, including fur between the toes. Confirm whether the brush insert is removable and how the cup drains. Check that there are no sharp seams or rough edges. If the seller does not provide dimensions, request them before ordering.

## Use gently and keep it dry

Introduce the cup while it is empty, then add a small amount of lukewarm water. Let the dog withdraw its paw freely. Rinse mud away without twisting or forcing the paw, then dry between the toes. Wash and dry the cup and insert fully between uses. Avoid using it over cuts or irritated skin; seek veterinary advice if a paw is injured or persistently inflamed.

## Bottom line

Choose by measured fit and simple cleaning, not by a generic "deep clean" claim. Stop if your dog resists.
