A foraging spinner may encourage a bird to work for small portions of food, but product suitability depends on species, size, and chewing behavior. The AliExpress title mentions multiple compartments; AutoPet has not inspected its materials or attachments.

## Check materials and mounting

Ask what the toy is made of, how it attaches to the cage, and whether the compartments can be removed for cleaning. Inspect for sharp edges, loose hardware, gaps where toes or beak could become trapped, and coatings that may chip. If the seller cannot answer material questions, choose a product recommended by an avian professional instead.

## Introduce with supervision

Start with an easy-to-reach reward and watch how the bird interacts with the toy. Keep the activity brief until the bird is comfortable. Remove it immediately if parts crack, loosen, or fray. Use only foods appropriate to the bird's usual diet and ask an avian veterinarian about dietary changes.

## Bottom line

Foraging should be enriching, not frustrating or hazardous. Match the toy to the bird and inspect it frequently.
