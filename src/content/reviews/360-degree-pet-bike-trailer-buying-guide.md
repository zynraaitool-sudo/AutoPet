## Separate stroller and bike modes

Ask whether the wheels swivel only with a stroller kit or whether they remain swiveling in every mode. Confirm whether the bicycle hitch is included and whether it requires a fixed-wheel, tracking-wheel, or removal procedure. The exact setup is not available in the source.

Compare the inside floor length and width with the pet. A wide entry does not establish a roomy cabin. Ask for standing and resting clearance, stated payload, folded dimensions, and restraint point. Verify tire size, wheel attachment, and replacement-part availability.

## Fit and option checks

Assess the bicycle, pet, and trailer as one setup. Check that the hitch does not interfere with brakes or frame parts and that the loaded trailer is workable on the planned route. Ask for the hitch standard and whether the exact bicycle has been confirmed.

Before buying, verify:

- Which configuration gives the swivel movement
- Whether stroller parts are included
- How wheels are locked or secured for cycling
- Hitch and connector details
- Cabin and folded dimensions
- Payload and restraint type
- Wheel-joint maintenance instructions

## First assembly and use

Inspect all pins, joints, wheels, and the hitch without a pet aboard. Test the trailer by walking and turning before cycling. Unless the manual says differently, set the bike connection exactly as shown and follow any requirement to lock or remove swiveling wheels. Introduce the pet while stationary and use a harness on the approved interior restraint.

Stop use if the pet shows distress, the wheels do not track, or the frame or hitch shifts. AutoPet has not independently tested this trailer.

## Common mistakes

- Leaving swivel wheels unlocked during cycling
- Assuming the stroller kit is included
- Treating a wide opening as enough cabin room
- Ignoring hitch fit for the exact bicycle
- Using a collar rather than a suitable harness restraint
