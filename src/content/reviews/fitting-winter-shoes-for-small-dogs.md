This AliExpress listing describes adjustable, cotton-lined winter shoes for small dogs, including Pomeranians, and claims a soft, non-slip sole. AutoPet has not tested the fit or traction. Do not assume that the seller's size label fits every dog in a breed.

## Measure each paw

Follow the seller's chart and measure each paw as instructed. Check whether the size is based on paw width, length, or both, and confirm how to measure weight-bearing paws if the seller specifies that method. If your measurements fall between sizes, ask the seller rather than guessing.

## Check the closure and sole

Look at how the shoe closes, whether it can be adjusted without constriction, and whether the sole is flexible enough for natural movement. The listing does not provide a verified traction or water-resistance test. Inspect seams and edges for areas that could rub.

## Start with a short indoor trial

Put on one shoe at a time and observe your dog's gait. Remove the shoes if they twist, slide, rub, or make the dog unwilling to walk. After a short trial, check each paw for redness, trapped debris, or dampness. Do not leave shoes on longer than the seller's instructions recommend.

Before buying, verify the selected pair, size chart, material details, care instructions, shipping, and returns. If your dog has a paw injury or persistent discomfort, seek veterinary guidance rather than using footwear to manage it.
