An illuminated collar can make a dog easier to notice during a low-light walk, but marketplace descriptions do not establish brightness or runtime. AutoPet has not measured this product's performance.

## Fit and power checks

Measure the dog's neck and compare the result with the actual adjustment range, allowing room for comfortable movement. Check whether the LED section is a full collar or an add-on, and confirm the connector, charging cable, and available light modes. Ask the seller how long charging takes and whether the collar can be cleaned or exposed to rain; do not infer water resistance from a photo.

## Use it as one layer

Charge and test the collar before a walk. Keep a separate, secure leash connection and use reflective clothing or other visibility tools as needed. Inspect the wiring, clasp, and strap regularly, and remove the collar if it becomes hot, damaged, or uncomfortable.

## Bottom line

Choose a size that fits first, then compare charging and care details. Treat the light as an extra visibility cue, not a safety guarantee.
