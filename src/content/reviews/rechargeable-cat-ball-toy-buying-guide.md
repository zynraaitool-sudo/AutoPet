## Plan for supervised play

Introduce the toy while you are present. Watch how your cat interacts with it and remove it if the toy cracks, sheds parts, becomes stuck, or causes distress. Inspect detachable pieces regularly; do not leave a damaged or loose-part toy within reach.

Follow the manufacturer's charging instructions. Keep the toy away from your pet while charging, and do not leave it charging unattended. If the seller does not provide clear battery and use instructions, ask before buying.
