This AliExpress listing describes a transparent capsule-style backpack for cats and small dogs and markets it as breathable. AutoPet has not tested the carrier. Confirm the selected model's measurements and construction before putting an animal inside.

## Check the usable space

Measure your pet in a natural sitting and standing posture. Compare those measurements with the interior space, not the outer shell. Ask for the seller's maximum pet weight and whether the floor is supported when the animal moves.

## Evaluate airflow and closures

Find the location and size of ventilation openings, the material around them, and the closures that prevent accidental opening. A transparent panel does not tell you how much air circulates or whether the carrier becomes warm in direct sun. Check strap stitching, zippers, and any internal tether instructions.

## Introduce the carrier at home

Place it on the floor with the entrance open and let the pet investigate voluntarily. Do not force a reluctant animal into a confined space. For short trial outings, observe posture, breathing, temperature, and signs of stress. End the trip if the pet appears uncomfortable.

## Verify travel rules separately

A product listing does not establish airline, public-transit, or vehicle approval. Measure the carrier and review the current rules for the transport provider. Confirm return terms and the exact options before ordering.
