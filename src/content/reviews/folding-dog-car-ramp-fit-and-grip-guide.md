A ramp may reduce the need to lift a dog into a vehicle, but fit and surface conditions matter. This article summarizes a seller listing, not a load, traction, or durability test.

## Measure before comparing listings

Measure the height from the ground to the vehicle entry point and the available space behind the vehicle. Compare these with the ramp's deployed length, walking width, and folded dimensions. Check the selected variant's stated weight limit and whether side rails, end hooks, or a carrying handle are included. Ask the seller how the ramp is secured to the vehicle and what surfaces are recommended.

## Inspect and introduce slowly

Before each use, set the ramp on a flat surface and check that its joints lock, its ends do not slide, and the walking surface is dry and intact. Guide the dog on and off at ground level before raising the ramp. Reward voluntary steps and stop if the dog appears uncertain, slips, or avoids the setup. A senior dog or one with pain may need veterinary guidance before changing how it enters a vehicle.

## Bottom line

Choose dimensions and secure attachment over marketing claims. Do not use a ramp that rocks, has damaged edges, or does not fit the vehicle.
