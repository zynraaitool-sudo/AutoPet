This guide is based on the AliExpress listing title for a portable dog water bottle. AutoPet has not inspected the materials or performed a leak test.

The title states a 285ml capacity and mentions stainless steel and silicone. Check which components use each material, the actual dimensions, how water is dispensed, and whether the selected option includes the parts shown in the listing. The seller's leak-resistant wording is not proof that the bottle will stay sealed in a bag.

## Before taking it outside

Follow the cleaning instructions and test the closure at home. Check that the seal sits correctly and that the bottle does not leak when stored in the position you plan to carry it. Recheck it regularly for wear.

Decide whether the capacity suits the dog and outing. Water needs vary with size, activity, weather, and trip length. Carry enough water and plan refills; do not treat a small portable bottle as a substitute for a suitable water supply on a long trip.

## Bottom line

The listing gives a stated capacity and materials to verify. Confirm the exact option, cleaning details, closure, and current delivery and return terms before buying.
