## Compare the measurements with your cat

Check the full length, width, height, and entrance opening. Compare the usable inner space with your cat's resting posture and the location where the bed will sit. Listing photos can distort scale, so rely on measurements rather than a model photo alone.

## Ask about materials and care

Look for the fabric composition, filling, removable covers, and washing instructions. A “winter” label does not provide a temperature rating or prove that a bed is suitable for every indoor climate. Find out whether the structure keeps its shape after routine cleaning.

## Choose a calm, accessible location

Set the bed on a stable surface where the cat can enter and leave without being cornered. Do not push a hesitant cat inside. Offer the bed alongside familiar resting places and allow the cat to decide whether to use it.

## Inspect it over time

Check seams, fabric, and any loose filling periodically. Remove the bed if it becomes unstable or damaged. Before ordering, confirm the color and design variant, dimensions, cleaning instructions, shipping, and return terms.
