This guide is based on an AliExpress flotation-vest listing. AutoPet has not tested buoyancy, stitching, or sizing, and a seller's "life jacket" label is not an independent safety certification.

## Fit the vest to the dog

Measure the chest and neck as instructed and compare them with the current chart. Confirm the adjustment range, closure layout, and whether a top handle is included. Check the seam quality and fabric condition when it arrives. The vest should not restrict breathing or movement; if the fit is uncertain, do not use it in water.

## Introduce water gradually

Let the dog wear the vest on dry land first, then introduce it in calm, shallow water while remaining close enough to assist. Watch for panic, fatigue, or difficulty keeping the head comfortably above water, and end the activity if any occurs. Never leave a dog unattended around a pool, dock, or boat.

## Bottom line

A well-fitted vest can be one precaution among several, not a promise of safety. Confirm measurements, inspect the product, and maintain direct supervision at all times.
