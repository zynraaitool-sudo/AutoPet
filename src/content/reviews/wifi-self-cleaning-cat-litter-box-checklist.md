An AliExpress listing for this product describes a large, automatic litter box with app control and 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi. Those are seller claims, not independently verified test results. AutoPet has not purchased or tested this unit.

## Confirm the exact model first

Listings may combine several sizes, colors, or configurations. Check the option selected at checkout and compare its dimensions with the space available at home. Ask which litter types and particle sizes are supported, whether the waste container is included, and what replacement parts are available.

## Check connectivity and offline behavior

“App control” does not tell you which functions require a phone, internet access, or an account. Look for the supported app name, phone requirements, router band, setup instructions, and what happens if the network is unavailable. Confirm whether the cleaning schedule can be stopped or changed locally.

## Put safety and gradual introduction first

Read the manual for entry sensors, cycle delays, emergency stops, and cleaning precautions. A product title cannot establish how the unit behaves if a cat enters during a cycle or if power is interrupted. Introduce an automatic system gradually while keeping a familiar litter tray available. Observe your cat using the device before relying on it.

## Plan for upkeep

Find out how the waste bin, rake or drum, and sensors are cleaned, and how often the seller recommends maintenance. Keep the area clean and check for stuck waste or changes in operation. If your cat avoids the box or shows a sudden change in litter habits, do not assume the appliance is the cause; seek appropriate veterinary advice.

Before ordering, compare the listing photos, selected variant, manual, return terms, and shipping. Do not rely on an app notification as proof that the box is clean or safe.

## Practical buying checklist

Use this guide to app-controlled cat litter boxes: confirm wi-fi and safety before buying by checking interior size, entry height, litter compatibility, sensor behavior, waste-bin access, power needs, and cleaning requirements. Verify the exact model or variant rather than relying on a category label or a feature in the listing title.

## Common mistake to avoid

A common mistake is choosing by exterior dimensions rather than usable interior space. Separate stated seller details from independently tested results, and confirm unresolved points in the live listing or manufacturer's documentation.
