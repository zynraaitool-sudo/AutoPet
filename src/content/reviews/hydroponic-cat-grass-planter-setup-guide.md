A small sprouting tray can make it easier to grow cat grass indoors, but it does not guarantee a healthy crop. The AliExpress listing describes a hydroponic-style tray; verify the exact package contents before ordering.

## Confirm the kit and routine

Check whether the chosen variant includes seeds, a growing mat, or only the plastic tray. Read the instructions for soaking, rinsing, drainage, and light. Choose a stable location where the cat cannot easily knock over the tray. Avoid standing water and clean the tray between growing cycles according to the seller's directions.

## Offer only healthy growth

Inspect sprouts daily and discard anything moldy, slimy, or foul-smelling. Do not use household chemicals on the growing surface unless the manufacturer confirms they are safe for this purpose. Introduce the grass in small amounts and observe the cat. If the animal has a health condition or unusual reaction, consult a veterinarian.

## Bottom line

The most important checks are package contents, drainage, cleaning, and freshness. A planter is a growing accessory, not a substitute for a complete diet or veterinary advice.
