## What to verify before buying

For either option, confirm the selected model, supported food and kibble size, capacity, scheduled meals, portion measurement, power requirements, backup behavior, cleaning instructions, and included parts. If more than one pet uses the feeder, consider whether they can access each other's meals.

For pets with a medical diet or a prescribed feeding plan, confirm the device's documented settings with your veterinarian before relying on it. Test a new feeder while you are at home, and continue checking that your pet is eating and has fresh water.
