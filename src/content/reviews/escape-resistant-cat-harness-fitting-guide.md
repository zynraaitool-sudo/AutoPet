The AliExpress listing describes a harness as escape-resistant, but that wording is not a guarantee. AutoPet has not tested the fit or fasteners. Cats differ in body shape and comfort with restraint, so suitability cannot be judged by a product photo.

## Measure and inspect

Use the seller's measurement instructions and compare chest and neck measurements with the size chart. Check the adjustment range, closure type, leash attachment, and which accessories are included. When the harness arrives, inspect stitching and buckles and fit it indoors. It should not restrict breathing or movement, but it should also not be loose enough to slip over the head or shoulders.

## Practice without pressure

Let the cat sniff the harness, reward calm investigation, and build up wearing time gradually. Add the leash indoors before trying an enclosed, quiet area. Do not leave a harnessed cat unattended or use the leash to lift the animal. Stop if the cat freezes, struggles, or appears distressed.

## Bottom line

Outdoor walking is optional. A well-fitted harness and the cat's comfort matter more than an "escape-proof" label.
