A suspended grooming hammock is a restraint accessory, not a guarantee of calm or safe nail care. The AliExpress listing describes a hammock for grooming; AutoPet has not assessed its fabric, sizing, or attachments.

## Assess before purchase

Use the seller's exact size chart and compare the animal's measurements, not just its weight. Confirm the number and placement of leg openings, whether hooks are included, and what secure support point is required. Inspect stitching and hardware when the item arrives. Do not attach it to a weak shelf, towel bar, or improvised support.

## Prioritize comfort and handling

Allow the cat to investigate the hammock while it is on the floor, then introduce handling in brief, positive steps. Never leave an animal suspended or unattended. Stop if the cat struggles, pants, freezes, vocalizes unusually, or shows other distress. If nail care is difficult, ask a veterinary professional or qualified groomer instead of forcing restraint.

## Bottom line

Only use a correctly sized, intact hammock with secure support and direct supervision. The cat's comfort determines whether this tool is appropriate.
