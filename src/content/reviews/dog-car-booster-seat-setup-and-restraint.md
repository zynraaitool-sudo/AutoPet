A booster seat may create a defined space for a small pet, but raised seating and crash protection are different claims. AutoPet has not tested the AliExpress product's anchors, stitching, or restraint performance.

## Check vehicle fit

Measure the seat area and compare it with the booster dimensions. Identify every strap and attachment point, confirm the seat can be secured without interfering with airbags or seat belts, and ask whether the internal tether is included. Check the fabric's cleaning directions and inspect seams when the item arrives.

## Treat restraint separately

The booster listing does not establish that it will protect a pet in a collision. Use only a restraint system appropriate to the vehicle and fitted harness, and never attach a tether to the dog's collar. Keep the pet from roaming between seats, and follow local rules for animals in vehicles.

## Bottom line

Choose the booster for measured fit and ease of installation, not an implied safety rating. Secure the dog appropriately and do not allow it to ride loose.
